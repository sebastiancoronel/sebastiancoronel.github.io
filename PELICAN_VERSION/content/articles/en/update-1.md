## What I'm building

The idea is simple: an application that helps me monitor my posture throughout
the day. The goal is something people can use every day without having to think
about it much.

That "without effort" is exactly the biggest challenge, and the whole series
comes from there.

## Where I am today

I'm in an uncomfortable but productive stage: after getting stuck on what I
consider the core of the app — the calibration — I managed to get it close to
what I wanted. It's not pretty yet, since I'm still figuring out which concept
to use for the UI, and it doesn't have everything I want, but it handles the
main case end to end.

What works today:

- The full main flow: someone can open the app, do what they came to do, and
  close it without getting stuck. That experience still needs polish.

## What's costing me the most

Being honest about this is the point:

1. **Focus.** Every week a better idea shows up. Sticking to one thing is the
   decision that costs me the most — nothing technical.
2. **"Almost ready."** The first 80% comes fast; the last 20% (the details that
   make something feel finished) takes longer than everything before it combined.
3. **A good UI.** It has to look good.

## What's next

- Improve the guided calibration. I need to tune the angle values and the
  inclination deltas for shoulders and ears.
